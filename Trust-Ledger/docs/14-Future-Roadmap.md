# Future Roadmap

**Project:** Trust Ledger  
**Document Version:** 1.0

---

# Overview

The current Proof of Concept demonstrates reusable KYC between Lloyds and Halifax. The architecture is designed to support future enhancements and additional participants.

---

# Phase 1 (Current PoC)

- Two Organizations (Lloyds & Halifax)
- Hyperledger Fabric Network
- Reusable KYC Credentials
- Credential Verification
- Credential Revocation
- React + NestJS Application

---

# Phase 2

- Add MBNA
- Add Bank of Scotland
- Multi-bank credential sharing
- Customer consent dashboard
- Enhanced audit reporting

---

# Phase 3

- Hyperledger Fabric CA
- Private Data Collections
- Multi-peer organizations
- High Availability deployment
- Kubernetes support

---

# Phase 4

- Decentralized Identifiers (DID)
- W3C Verifiable Credentials
- Digital Identity Wallet
- Zero Knowledge Proofs (ZKP)
- Open Banking Integration

---

# Long-Term Vision

Trust Ledger can evolve into a trusted digital identity platform that enables secure identity verification across banks, financial institutions, and regulated organizations.

Potential future participants include:

- Banks
- Insurance Providers
- Government Agencies
- Credit Bureaus
- FinTech Companies
